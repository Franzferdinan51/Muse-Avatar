# Collection credits

Every avatar in `collection/` lists where it came from. Pulled art
keeps its credit here; Juno's own generations say so.

## hermes-ink.png / hermes-ink-white.png

"Hermes" - the ink girl (Nous girl) from Duckets' own Hermes
projects on his Mac:

- Black ink version: `HermesDesktopBuddy/app/src/main/res/drawable-xxxhdpi/hermes_avatar.png`
- White version: `HermesGadget/assets/mascot/nous-girl-white-1024.png`

Art from the Hermes / Nous Research ecosystem, archived here by the
owner for personal avatar use. Converted to 192x192 pixel sprites
(4 coverage levels, transparent background) by Juno with
`tools/make_hermes_ink.py`. The white version reads on dark
backdrops; the black ink is for light ones.

## juno-hires.png

"Juno, portrait" - an original by Juno: the duck in profile at
128x128 with gradient shading, generated by `tools/make_juno_hires.py`.
No external source.

## juno-bubble.png

"Juno, bubble" - the glossy Juno brand logo from Duckets' own
MuseCompanionApp on his Mac:

- Source: `MuseCompanionApp/app/assets/brand/juno-logo-glossy.png`
  (1616x1584) - Juno the black duck in sunglasses inside a blue
  water bubble.

Brand art from the owner's own MuseCompanionApp project, archived
here by the owner for personal avatar use. Converted to a 192x192
full-colour sprite (circle-cropped to the bubble, smooth
anti-aliased rim, transparent background) by Juno with
`tools/make_juno_bubble.py`.

## hermes-knight.png

"Hermes, knight" - the chibi Hermes knight mascot (winged helmet,
blue-and-gold armour, caduceus staff) from the Hermes desktop app
assets in Duckets' local project tree on his Mac:

- Source: `Projects/Grok-Build-Desktop-App-work/upstream/hermes/apps/desktop/public/hermes.png`

Art from the Hermes / Nous Research ecosystem, archived here by the
owner for personal avatar use. Converted to a 192x192 full-colour

## juno-studio.png

"Juno, studio" - the rendered Juno (black duck, suit, sunglasses)
from Duckets' own MuseCompanionApp on his Mac:

- Source: `MuseCompanionApp/app/assets/avatar/juno_idle.mp4`
  (720x720, 24fps) - frame at t=8.5s, head turned three-quarter,
  bill in profile. The same art the watch's GIF face is built from.

Companion-app art from the owner's own project, archived here by
the owner for personal avatar use. Converted to a 192x192
full-colour sprite (studio backdrop flood-keyed to transparency,
