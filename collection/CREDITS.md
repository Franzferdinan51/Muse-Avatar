# Collection credits

Every avatar in `collection/` lists where it came from. Pulled art
keeps its credit here; Juno's own generations say so.

## hermes-ink.png / hermes-ink-white.png

"Hermes" - the ink girl (Nous girl) from Duckets' own Hermes
projects on his Mac:

- Black ink version: `HermesDesktopBuddy/app/src/main/res/drawable-xxxhdpi/hermes_avatar.png`
- White version: `HermesGadget/assets/mascot/nous-girl-white-1024.png`

Art from the Hermes / Nous Research ecosystem, archived here by the
owner for personal avatar use. Converted to 192x192 pixel sprites
(4 coverage levels, transparent background) by Juno with
`tools/make_hermes_ink.py`. The white version reads on dark
backdrops; the black ink is for light ones.
